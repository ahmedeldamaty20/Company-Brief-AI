# Hugging Face — The AI community building the future

Hugging Face is the collaboration platform at the heart of the machine learning community. It brings together models, datasets, spaces, and tools to create, discover, and deploy open-source AI.

## Who we are
- Founded in 2016, with a mission to solve and democratize artificial intelligence through natural language.
- Headquarters and activity centered in Paris (with a strong presence in NYC) and a global, collaborative community reaching across the world.
- We empower the next generation of machine learning engineers, scientists, and end users to learn, collaborate, and share their work to build an open and ethical AI future together.

Brand essence:
- “The AI community building the future.”
- The Hugging Face Hub is a central place where anyone can share, explore, discover, and experiment with open-source ML.
- A vibrant ecosystem that hosts some of the most widely used open-source ML libraries and tools, all crafted by a talented science team pushing the edge of technology.

## What we do
- Build and nurture an open, collaborative platform for ML: Models, Datasets, Spaces, and Buckets.
- Provide enterprise-grade solutions and support to help teams deploy and operate AI at scale.
- Offer a comprehensive suite of products and resources:
  - Models: Browse 2M+ models from a global community
  - Datasets: Access 500k+ datasets
  - Spaces: Create and run AI apps and demos
  - Inference Endpoints: Deploy and scale models in production
  - Storage Buckets: Store and manage data and artifacts
  - HuggingChat: Conversational AI capabilities
  - Documentation, Learn resources, and Community forums
  - Hugging Face PRO and Enterprise Support for professional teams
  - Inference Providers and other enterprise-friendly offerings

What customers and partners experience:
